HOW TO PLAY

Player 1 — A/D move · W jump · S crouch/block · J light · K heavy · U throw · L special · Space dodge roll · P pause

Player 2 — Arrow keys move · N light · M heavy · , special · . throw · / dodge roll

Moves — J→J→J chains · Down+J low kick · Down+K sweep (knockdown) · Up+K anti-air launch · Forward+K lunge · Air+J knee · Air+K dive · Throw beats block · Tap back just before impact to Parry · Blocking fills the Guard gauge — a full gauge means Guard Break.

Meter — land hits to fill the blue Shadow Meter. At full, L unleashes your fighter's Shadow Special.

Training — H toggles the hitbox overlay · dummy behaviour from the Dojo menu · infinite timer, HP regenerates.
